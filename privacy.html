Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Glacier Tube (com.glacier.tube, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Contact: sk5299746@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • You can use the app without signing in.
  • Signing in is optional and is used for your account and YouTube integration.
  • Watch history and settings are stored on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • YouTube actions (likes, comments, subscriptions) happen only when you request them.

2. Information we handle

2.1 Account and sign-in data

If you choose to sign in with Google, authentication is processed by Supabase Auth. We receive and store the account details needed to show your profile: email address, display name, profile photo URL, and authentication tokens. These values are stored in the app's private storage on your device.

2.2 YouTube connection

If you connect your YouTube account, Google gives the app an OAuth access token and refresh token. With your permission, the app can read and modify YouTube data on your behalf, including:

  • your channel name, channel ID, avatar, and public subscriber count;
  • your playlists, Watch Later list, and Liked videos list;
  • playlist items and related video details;
  • subscriptions, ratings (likes/dislikes), and comments that you choose to create or remove.

YouTube requests are made directly from your device to Google. The app does not send your YouTube tokens to our own servers. Tokens and cached channel details are stored in the app's private storage and are removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

2.3 Watch history and playback positions

The app stores a local watch history on your device. This can include video IDs, titles, channel names, thumbnails, the time you watched a video, its duration, and your last playback position. The history is limited to the most recent entries and can be cleared from the app's History screen.

2.4 Settings

Preferences such as language, region, video quality, autoplay, data saving, notifications, and shorts rotation are stored locally on your device.

2.5 Technical request data

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. When the app loads content from third-party services, those services receive standard technical information such as your IP address, user agent, and the requested URL, as is normal for any internet request.

3. How we use information

  • To sign you in and display your account.
  • To connect your YouTube channel and show your library.
  • To perform YouTube actions you explicitly request.
  • To remember your settings and resume videos where you left off.
  • To keep the app secure, diagnose failures, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, share it with data brokers, or use it for advertising. We do not use your YouTube data for any purpose other than providing the features you request.

4. YouTube API Services

Glacier Tube uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can review or revoke the app's access to your Google Account at any time at Google Account permissions. Revoking access stops future YouTube API requests, but does not automatically delete data already stored on your device.

5. Third-party services

To provide video browsing, playback, and related metadata, the app may contact the following categories of services. Each service processes requests under its own privacy policy:

  • Google and YouTube — authentication, the YouTube Data API, video playback, and content metadata.
  • Supabase — account authentication.
  • Community YouTube front-end APIs such as Piped, Invidious, and Hoshiyomi — public feed and stream metadata.
  • SponsorBlock and Return YouTube Dislike — optional sponsor segments and public rating data.
  • OMDb and TMDB — movie and TV metadata used for some catalogue entries.

These services receive only the request data needed to answer the app's requests. We do not control their privacy practices.

6. Storage and security

Account tokens, the connected channel, watch history, and settings are stored in app-private storage on your device. We do not operate a separate database that stores your watch history or YouTube tokens. No method of storage is completely secure; please protect your device with a screen lock and keep your Google account secure.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Watch history: kept on your device until you clear it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • YouTube tokens and channel details: removed when you sign out of the app or uninstall it. You can also revoke access from your Google Account.
  • Account data processed by Supabase: retained while your account exists. Contact us to request deletion of your app account; we may need to verify your identity.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your account data, email sk5299746@gmail.com.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without signing in.
  • Disconnect YouTube or sign out from the Account screen.
  • Clear watch history from the History screen.
  • Revoke Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your account data by contacting us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable data protection laws, including the right to object to processing or lodge a complaint with your local authority.

9. Children

Glacier Tube is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. International processing

Google, YouTube, Supabase, and the other services listed above may process data in countries other than yours. Their privacy policies describe the safeguards they use.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
